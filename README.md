Data scientist at the New York City Council's Oversight & Investigations Division.

Selected work:

- [council-discourse](https://github.com/umqadir/council-discourse) - every NYC Council meeting transcribed with named speakers, chaptered, and published within a day, after Vikram Oberoi's citymeetings.nyc. Live at [council-discourse.pages.dev](https://council-discourse.pages.dev).
- [crimerisk](https://github.com/umqadir/crimerisk) - national block-group-level crime-risk surface for the US, built from public data.
- [lobbying-signals](https://github.com/umqadir/lobbying-signals) - trend detection over federal lobbying disclosures. Live at [umqadir.github.io/lobbying-signals](https://umqadir.github.io/lobbying-signals).
- [ecg-clinical](https://github.com/umqadir/ecg-clinical) - do diagnostic models still work at hospitals they weren't trained at? ECG models trained at one hospital, measured on how much they lose at two others.
- [small-lm-lab](https://github.com/umqadir/small-lm-lab) - training small language models from scratch to pinpoint the moment they learn to copy patterns from context.
- [nettalk](https://github.com/umqadir/nettalk) - from-scratch NumPy reproduction of NETtalk (Sejnowski & Rosenberg, 1987).
